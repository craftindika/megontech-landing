About Megon

Building Digital Infrastructure for Hospitality in Northeast India.

We help hotels, resorts, and restaurants move from manual operations to modern, connected systems. While supporting them in growing their business.

Who We Are

Who we are.

Megon Technologies India Pvt. Ltd. is a Guwahati-based travel technology company building digital infrastructure for the hospitality industry in Northeast India.

In a region where many hotels still rely on manual systems, we create simple, connected software that brings bookings, billing, and guest management into one place—reducing errors and improving daily operations.

Our focus is to make technology practical, reliable, and easy to use for every property.
