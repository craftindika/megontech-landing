Why Most Hotels in Guwahati Still Don't Use PMS (And What It's Costing Them)

SK

Supratim Kashyap

June 16, 2026 · 10 min read

The hospitality industry across Guwahati, Shillong, Cherrapunji, Kaziranga, Dimapur, Dibrugarh, Jorhat, Silchar, and Agartala is expanding rapidly. Tourism in Assam and Northeast India is at an all-time high, but a large number of hotels, resorts, and homestays are still operating without a hotel management software (PMS).

Despite the growth of online bookings and OTAs like MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, and Agoda, many properties continue to rely on manual systems, leading to hidden revenue loss and operational inefficiencies.

If you're running a hotel in Guwahati or anywhere in Northeast India, this guide explains:

  • Why hotels still don't adopt PMS
  • The real cost of not using hotel software
  • Why switching to a hotel management system in Assam and Northeast India is now essential

Hotel Demand Across Assam & Northeast India (Beyond Tourism)

Hospitality demand in Northeast India is no longer limited to tourist destinations, it spans business hubs, transit cities, and emerging travel circuits.

Key Hotel Markets Driving PMS Demand

  • Guwahati (Assam) — Gateway to Northeast India with year-round occupancy
  • Shillong & Cherrapunji (Meghalaya) — High tourism-driven bookings
  • Kaziranga (Assam) — Seasonal resort demand with peak load challenges
  • Dimapur & Kohima (Nagaland) — Commercial + event-based hotel demand (Hornbill Festival surge)
  • Dibrugarh, Jorhat & Tinsukia (Upper Assam) — Tea industry, corporate stays, transit travel
  • Silchar (Barak Valley) — Dense hotel ecosystem with low digital adoption
  • Itanagar / Naharlagun (Arunachal Pradesh) — Government and official travel
  • Agartala (Tripura) — Growing urban hospitality segment

Across these cities, thousands of properties still operate without a hotel PMS in Northeast India, creating a massive gap in operational efficiency.

Tourism Growth: Why PMS Adoption is Urgent

  • Assam attracts 6–8 million domestic tourists annually, with Guwahati as the entry point
  • Kaziranga National Park sees 200,000+ visitors yearly, creating peak season pressure
  • Shillong & Cherrapunji are among India's fastest-growing eco-tourism destinations
  • Nagaland's Hornbill Festival drives massive short-term hotel demand
  • Arunachal Pradesh circuits (Tawang, Ziro) are gaining premium travelers

Hotels without a hotel management software in Guwahati, Shillong, or Kaziranga struggle to manage demand, pricing, and bookings efficiently.

The Ground Reality: Most Hotels Still Operate Manually

Across cities like Guwahati, Dimapur, Dibrugarh, Shillong, and Silchar, many hotels still use:

  • Paper registers for bookings
  • Excel sheets for room allocation
  • Manual billing systems
  • No OTA synchronization

This leads to inefficiencies, especially as online bookings increase across Northeast India.

Why Hotels in Guwahati & Northeast India Still Don't Use PMS

1. "Hotel Software is Too Expensive"

Many hotel owners assume that hotel management software in Assam or Guwahati is costly.

Reality: Modern cloud-based PMS solutions are affordable and designed for small hotels, resorts, and homestays in Northeast India.

2. Lack of Awareness in Key Markets

In cities like:

  • Silchar
  • Dimapur
  • Jorhat

Many hotel operators are still unfamiliar with:

  • PMS functionality
  • OTA integrations
  • Revenue optimization tools

3. Comfort with Traditional Systems

Hotels in Guwahati, Shillong, and Dibrugarh that have operated for years manually often believe "our current system works fine."

This hides significant inefficiencies and revenue leakage.

4. Internet & Infrastructure Concerns

In regions like:

  • Kaziranga
  • Arunachal Pradesh
  • Interior Nagaland

Hotels worry about connectivity and system reliability.

5. Staff Training Limitations

Small hotels and homestays across Northeast India often operate with limited staff, making them hesitant to adopt new systems.

What It's Costing Hotels (Hidden Losses)

1. Revenue Loss During Peak Demand

During high-demand periods:

  • Kaziranga safari season
  • Shillong & Cherrapunji peak tourism
  • Hornbill Festival (Nagaland)

Hotels without a PMS in Northeast India:

  • Fail to manage inventory
  • Lose bookings due to manual errors

2. Overbookings from OTA Mismanagement

Hotels in Guwahati, Dimapur, and Shillong without a PMS often:

  • Double-book rooms
  • Fail to update availability across OTAs

3. Lower Occupancy in Off-Season

Without a hotel management system in Assam, hotels lack:

  • Demand insights
  • Pricing strategies

This is especially critical in seasonal markets like Kaziranga and Cherrapunji.

4. Operational Inefficiency

Manual processes increase workload for staff in:

  • Front desk operations
  • Billing
  • Reporting

5. Compliance Risks

Hotels across Assam, Nagaland, and Arunachal Pradesh must maintain guest records. Without a digital PMS system:

  • Errors increase
  • Compliance becomes difficult

6. Poor Guest Experience

Modern travelers visiting Shillong boutique stays, Kaziranga resorts, and Guwahati business hotels expect:

  • Fast check-in
  • Accurate bookings
  • Digital billing

Manual systems fail to deliver.

Key Takeaway

The barriers to PMS adoption in Northeast India are mostly perception, not reality. Cost, connectivity, and training fears are all solvable, while the hidden losses from manual operations — lost peak-season bookings, OTA overbookings, and compliance gaps — keep compounding every month.
