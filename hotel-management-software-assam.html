Core Capabilities

Everything You Need in One Simple System.

Four pillars of Reservista PMS. Tap any to see it in action.

1/4
01 · Booking Management

Handle every reservation from one calendar.

Walk-ins, OTA bookings, peak-season blocks — your full pipeline in a single timeline you can drag, filter, and act on.

  • Multi-channel sync — Booking.com, MMT, Agoda, direct
  • Drag to reschedule across rooms or dates
  • Colour-coded status — confirmed, tentative, cancelled
02 · Front Desk Operations

A live list for arrivals, departures, and in-house guests.

No more switching screens or chasing registers. Your front desk sees who's coming, who's leaving, and who's currently checked in — all in real time.

  • Mobile check-in from a phone or tablet, no PC required
  • Auto GST invoice generated on check-out
  • Guest profile with stay history, preferences, ID proof
03 · Room Management

Every room's status — at a single glance.

A live grid of your full property. Occupied, vacant, cleaning, maintenance — colour-coded and synced across the front desk, housekeeping and your phone.

  • Real-time grid across all floors and room types
  • Housekeeping status updated from any device
  • Block or release rooms with one tap
04 · Reports & Insights

Numbers that tell you what to do next.

Daily revenue, ADR, occupancy, RevPAR. Compare today vs last week. Spot the dip before it becomes a trend — and export anything to share with your team or accountant.

  • Daily metrics — revenue, ADR, occupancy, RevPAR
  • Compare any window — today, week, month, year
  • Export to CSV or share to WhatsApp in one tap
